Every score is computed from the code in `dr/`, the log files generated by **your own** drill run, and the three reports in `reports/`. A number that doesn't trace back to a real timestamp does not count as evidence.

## Hard-Fail Conditions

These fail the submission **regardless of total score**:

1. **`tools/measure_rto.py` returns `"valid":false`** for the Step 4 drill. Common causes: a forced double outage (`--i-really-want-both`), killing a region while the other was already down, no failed request after the kill, the load generator and chaos script not overlapping in time, or "recovery" still being served by the region you killed.
   Gate: `pytest tests/test_rto_evidence.py::test_drill2_hop_le`, `::test_rto_do_duoc_bang_timestamp`, `::test_chaos_khong_giet_ca_hai_region`.

2. **RTO in `reports/rto-evidence.md` differs by more than 1 second** from the `rto_measured_s` computed by `tools/measure_rto.py` — or the RPO table doesn't match the logs.
   Gate: `pytest tests/test_rto_evidence.py::test_so_trong_bang_khop_voi_so_trong_log`.

> `test_drill2_hop_le` also requires an empty `warnings` list. In particular, a cutover that happens *before* the health checker actually detects the outage (`t_cutover < t_detect`) must be fixed by re-running the automation correctly — it cannot be submitted as a clean drill.
